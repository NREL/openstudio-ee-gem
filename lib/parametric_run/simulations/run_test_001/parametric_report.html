Executive Summary

This report compares energy consumption and operational costs between baseline and all applied renovation scenarios from CSV results. Embodied carbon is annualized using an analysis period of 30 years, while operational carbon is reported over 1 year. The construction cost data is from RSMeans Database. The embodied carbon is calculated using data from EC3 Environmental Product Declaration (EPD) Database.
